ABOUT US


One oven, one
family, one street.

Pizza Palace started as a single wood-fired oven built by
hand in a garage. It's grown since, but the oven — and the
philosophy behind it — hasn't changed.
Rosa Bellini

FOUNDER & HEAD BAKER

"I didn't want to open a resturant. I wanted to build the oven my
grandfather described to me, and see what came out of it."




HOW WE GOT HERE


A short history of a long dough proof.


2014

The garage oven Rosa hand-builds a wood-fired dome in her parents' garage, working nights off
a recipe passed down from her grandfather in Naples.





2016

Farmers market stand Weekend pop-ups sell out within the hour. Word spreads mostly by the people
bringing friends back the following week.





2019

1420 Main Street Opens The current shop opens with the same oven, moved brick by brick from the
garage to its permanent home.





2026

Still doing it the slow way Same 48-hour dough, same local farm, now with online ordering so the line
moves a little faster.












WHAT WE WON'T COMPROMISE ON


Three rules the kitchen doesn't break.
Slow dough, always
Every batch ferments a minimum of 48 hours. If
we sell out, we sell out — we don't rush the next
batch to cover it. 		Farms we've meet
Produce and cheese come from three farms
within 40 miles, all visited in person before we put
them on the menu. 		Nothing frozen
No frozen dough, no pre-shredded cheese, no
bottled sauce. If it can't be made fresh that day, it
isn't served.
