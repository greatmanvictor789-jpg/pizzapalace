WHY IT TASTES DIFFERENT


We only changed the parts that mattered.

Most places speed up the dough to save time. We slow it down. Cold
fermentation is the difference between a crust that's just a base for
toppings and one that's worth eating on its own — blistered, a little
chewey, faintly sour. It's slower, and it's the whole point.

Everything else follows from the same instinct: fewer toppings, better
ones, put on with restraint so that the crust never gets soggy carrying them.
